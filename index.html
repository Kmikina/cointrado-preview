FAQ

Is this real?

No. CoinTrado is a demo prototype. No real money, rates or accounts.

What does it cost?

A disclosed spread shown on every quote, plus network fees when you send on-chain — both shown before you confirm.

Can I use it in French?

Yes — toggle EN/FR on the site and in app settings.

Why do you need my ID?

Canadian anti-money-laundering rules require identity verification for crypto dealers. We tell you exactly what's collected and why.
